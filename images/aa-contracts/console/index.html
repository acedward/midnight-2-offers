Midnight Demo Console

Your browser EVM wallet signs EIP-712 actions; the stack's relay proves them (~1–2 min) through the compose proof server and submits to Midnight. The wallet never sees a Midnight key.

Any injected EIP-1193 wallet works (MetaMask, Rabby, …). It only ever signs typed data — no chain, no gas, no funds needed on the EVM side.

Register account

Creates an AA account owned by the connected EVM address (fresh random salt → fresh account id). Signed by your wallet, executed by the relay.

Actions — the Manager's execute

Selector 5 — internal transfer between AA accounts, signed by the sender's EVM wallet.

Selector 4 — internal SHIELDED transfer between AA accounts, signed by the sender's EVM wallet.

Selector 3 — withdraw the account's unshielded balance to any standard Midnight address (a 32-byte user address; contract recipients are refused by design).

—

Selector 2 — withdraw the account's SHIELDED balance to a wallet. The recipient's coin public key rides the signed action; their encryption key is resolved at build time (which is why the recipient is one of the stack's wallets here).

Selector 6 — opens a shielded swap on the Manager, signed by your EVM wallet. The proven transaction is never submitted — unbalanced by exactly +give/−want, it IS the offer, published to the offer-files kernel as a swapoffer1… blob for a taker (or the solver) to settle. The give leg spends the account's shielded balance — fund it first via Fund account → Fund shielded above.

Read & pure functions

The Manager's non-mutating surface: pure functions run the compiled circuit module locally, read functions are ledger lookups through the indexer. No signature, no proof, instant.

This is the layout for a Midnight-wallet-controlled AA account: the same Manager, but the account's owner authorizes actions with a Midnight wallet (Lace / dapp-connector or a local seed) instead of an EVM signature. Nothing on this tab performs any operation yet — it exists so the UI can be adjusted before the logic is wired. Solver feed

The ported COW solver runs in observation mode: it mirrors the kernel's book and pushes its price ladders over the authenticated relay WebSocket to a demo-side sink, which this view streams live (same-origin SSE). The sink implements only the receive half of the relay boundary — it can never dispatch work, so the solver can never build, prove, or submit a transaction.
